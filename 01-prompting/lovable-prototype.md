https://lovable.dev/projects/085e54c0-8cb1-4626-9999-04328fe3cf07

## What it demonstrates

Juno demonstrates the ability to take raw, messy customer feedback and automatically turns that information into organized, prioritized insights (high, medium, low with sentiment tags), then draft a structured plan (PRD) from those insights. All this without me having to manually sort through the raw data first.

## Debrief

- **What worked:** I noticed that Juno used color coding effectively. For example the red/high priority tags with negative sentiment made the biggest problems easy to spot. The draft PRD highlighted key words in bold, making the document easy to scan quickly.
- **What broke / felt like a toy:** Juno labeled the navigation bar complaint with serious-sounding words like 'regression,' making a casual comment about eye strain sound like an official, bigger problem than it was. It also seemed to completely skip Sarah's dark mode request instead of noting it as a minor, low-priority item.
- **What I'd change next pass:** Next time, I'd tell Juno to only use serious technical words like 'regression' when there's real evidence backing it up, not just one person's opinion. I'd also tell Juno to never fully drop a request even small ones like dark mode should stay listed, just marked as low priority.
